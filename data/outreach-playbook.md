# PayRecover Cold Outreach Playbook

## Strategy
Target: small agencies (2-50 people) with recurring invoicing.
Hook: they know late payments hurt, they've never had a system.
Angle: recover locked-up cash flow without awkward client conversations.

## Sequence (email only, 3 touches)

### Touch 1 — Day 0 (problem intro)

Subject: [Name], quick question about late payments

Hi [First],

I was looking at [Agency] and noticed you run [client type] work. Question for
you: what happens when a client sits on an invoice 30+ days?

Most agency owners I talk to say the same thing — they know they should follow
up, but the follow-up feels awkward, so they delay. Meanwhile payroll doesn't
wait.

We built PayRecover to fix exactly this. It watches your open invoices and
sends polite, professional follow-ups to late payers in your voice — escalating
only when needed. Set it up once, never chase again.

Worth a 15-min look? Happy to show you the system with your own invoices.

[Your name]
PayRecover

## Rules
- Always use the agency's real first name
- Never pitch before proving you know their business
- Reference a stat or their specific situation in touch 2
- Keep it short — 5 lines max per email
- Track opens and replies; follow up with phone/voice if they open 2+ times

## Sending infrastructure (needs Antonio)
- Domain: payrecover.com (or .io) — $12/year
- Email: Google Workspace or Zoho — free/cheap
- Sender: Resend or Postmark for deliverability (free tier to start)
- Tool: send from a real inbox, 20-30 emails/day per address max
